Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Clear answers before you reserve your appointment.

Do I need to wash or dry my hair before coming?

No. The salon can handle the washing, drying and preparation for your service.

Are hair extensions included?

Sometimes. Each Square service description will clearly explain whether extensions are included.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit is required to secure the appointment.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Please contact the salon as early as possible. Deposits may be refunded according to the salon's cancellation policy.

What should I do if I am running late?

Call or message the salon as soon as possible so Rosa can confirm whether the appointment can still be accommodated.

Do you accept children?

Yes. Children are welcome, and age-appropriate styles are available.

Can I choose a different color or length?

Yes, depending on availability. Some colors or lengths may require an additional charge.

Should I send a reference photo?

It is recommended, especially for a custom style, but it is not required.
